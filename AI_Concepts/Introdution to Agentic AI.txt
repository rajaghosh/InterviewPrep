What are AI Agents?
Ans - AI Agents are autonomous reasoning engines (e.g the LLM) that can understand context,
 plan workflows, connect to external tools and data, and execute multi-step actions to 
 achieve a defined goal - with minimal human intervention.

------------------

AI Paradigm

Different paradigms of AI learn from different complexities of data from human features, to 
raw data, to dynamically observed.

a. AI - It is the capacity for machines to perform human-like tasks.

b. ML - It trains models to identify and predict patterns based on human processed data.
e.g Predictive maintenance from time-series signals.

c. DL (Deep Learning) - It is a powerfil paradigm that uses neural network models to 
    learn pattern from raw data. This is specific to high volume of data. All LLMs are
    developed using the deep learning.
e.g Cancer detection in CT scans.

d. Generative AI - It is a subset of AI using large models to learn patterns well enough
to create new data
e.g Simple customer chatbot.

Agentic AI - It involves autonomous agaents that take actions and can learn to react based 
on feedback information from their environment in a multi-step manner.
e.g Strategic planning or code writing

To achieve the highest level of automation every major org target is to reach AGI. Where 
we can transfer all the repeatative task to a machine so we can work on th task we are interested in.
