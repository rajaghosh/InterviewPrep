## PHASE 1: Arrays, Strings & Basics (Day 1–20)

### Week 1 (Day 1–7) – Arrays Fundamentals

- **Day 1** – Array basics, traversal, input/output
- **Day 2** – Time & Space Complexity (Big-O basics)
- **Day 3** – Find max/min, second largest element
- **Day 4** – Reverse array, check sorted array
- **Day 5** – Left/Right rotation of array
- **Day 6** – Solve 5–6 easy array problems
- **Day 7** – Revision + re-solve weak problems

### Week 2 (Day 8–14) – Advanced Arrays

- **Day 8** – Prefix Sum concept
- **Day 9** – Kadane's Algorithm (max subarray)
- **Day 10** – Stock Buy & Sell problems
- **Day 11** – Majority Element (Moore Voting)
- **Day 12** – Two Sum (Hashing intro)
- **Day 13** – Sliding Window technique
- **Day 14** – Revision + medium problems

### Week 3 (Day 15–20) – Strings + Hashing

- **Day 15** – String basics, palindrome
- **Day 16** – Anagram, frequency count
- **Day 17** – HashMap & Set deep dive
- **Day 18** – Longest substring without repeating
- **Day 19** – Pattern-based string problems
- **Day 20** – Full revision + mixed practice
